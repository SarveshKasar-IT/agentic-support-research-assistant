Customer Support Knowledge Base

Account and Login

Customers can reset their password from the login page by selecting "Forgot Password".
A password reset link is sent to the registered email address.
If the customer does not receive the email, they should check their spam or junk folder.

Orders

Customers can check order status from the Orders section of their account.
An order marked as Processing has been received and is being prepared.
An order marked as Shipped has been handed to the delivery carrier.
An order marked as Delivered has reached the customer.

Returns

Customers can request a return from the Orders section.
Items must normally be returned within the applicable return window.
The customer should provide the order number and reason for the return.

Refunds

Refunds are normally initiated after the returned item has been received and inspected.
The time required for the refund to appear depends on the payment method and financial institution.

Shipping

Customers can track shipped orders using the tracking information available in their order details.
Delivery times may vary depending on the destination and shipping provider.

Technical Support

If a customer experiences repeated login problems, they should first reset their password.
If the problem continues after a password reset, the issue should be escalated to technical support.

Escalation

Issues involving suspected unauthorized account access, repeated payment failures, or unresolved technical problems should be escalated to the appropriate support team.
